# Transferring tiny-fleet lessons to mishe-tauftauf

## Decision

Transfer the **evaluation method**, not the current adapter weights or an assertion that routing wins. Tiny-fleet's registered question concerns 360M adapters; Mishe routes work among resident minds with prompts, tools, permissions and histories. Those are different interventions. Offline passage likelihood is not evidence of better issue resolution, safe actions, or cheaper orchestration.

The immediate reusable contribution is `scripts/audit_study_independence.py`: measure split/source-label collisions and normalized example overlap, bind inputs by hash, and retain explicit limitations. Its numeric-template diagnostic is a warning about repeated forms, not an independent-source estimator or proof of contamination. A Mishe task corpus needs the same discipline but its own schema and ancestry audit.

## What transfers, and what does not

| Tiny-fleet lesson | Mishe application | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A specialist must beat strong controls | Compare role dispatch against a generalist and task-specific prompting | Same initial task snapshot, actual isolated executions |
| Distinct case labels need not imply independent sources | Split repository, incident, task-template and time families before evaluation | Original ancestry; no sibling family across train/calibration/test |
| A best-answer oracle is not a router | Log role choice before answers; evaluate abstention and fallback | Pinned dispatch rule and per-episode decision tape |
| Generation seeds do not create independent training replicas | Repeated wakes do not create independent task episodes | Family-level paired analysis; complete episode denominators |
| PPL, exact match, executable correctness and style are different outcomes | Artifact existence, healthy processes and task success are different outcomes | Deterministic acceptance checks or blinded independent judgments |
| Cost must include failed attempts | Include retries, idle wakes, reviewer effort and failed episodes | Total wall/model/tool costs per episode, no duplicated receipts |
| A synthetic refusal is not an observed safety decision | Preserve permission boundaries and measure unsafe attempts separately | Realistic threat cases; external actions remain gated |

## Prospective shadow experiment

This is a design, **not a completed experiment**. Register it separately before outcomes; never amend `fleet-study-v1` to accommodate it.

1. **Question:** does deterministic role dispatch improve independently verified completion at acceptable total cost compared with a single generalist?
2. **Units:** licensed/redacted task snapshots from independent repository/incident/template families, split before normalization. Determine sample size from a declared effect or precision target. Count one episode once, regardless of wake count.
3. **Arms:** generalist; generalist with task-specific instruction; deterministic charter dispatch. A learned dispatch arm requires its own training/calibration split and frozen router. An output-selected best role is only an oracle diagnostic.
4. **Controls:** identical initial snapshots, model/tool capabilities, permission policy and declared context/cost budgets. Pin and hash revisions, prompts/charters, router, inputs and acceptance checks. Isolate histories and writable worktrees across arms. Retain the operator-required resident model; a model substitution is a separate intervention.
5. **Execution:** actually perform each task in a sandbox. Record dispatch before output, role, abstention/escalation, outputs, errors and timestamps. An invitation, saved answer or healthy pane does not establish completion. Shadow execution causes no external side effects; simulated effects cannot establish production safety.
6. **Outcomes:** independently verified completion, regressions, unsafe attempted actions, abstention and useful coverage, separately by task category. Blind arm labels for subjective ratings; report reviewer agreement. Retain missing/failed/capped cases and their denominators.
7. **Cost:** cumulative wall time, model tokens and available charges, tool calls, retries and review effort per episode. Separate unavailable accounting from zero cost. Count duplicated batch or wake receipts once.
8. **Analysis:** paired family-level differences and uncertainty. Freeze quality, safety, coverage and cost gates against the actual operational threat model. Do not transplant fleet's numerical thresholds merely because they exist.
9. **Promotion:** independent scientific review, then a bounded canary with rollback and operator-authorized external actions. Preserve permission gates; negative or inconclusive outcomes prohibit a superiority claim.

## Current adoption boundary

The local tiny-fleet plant can immediately prioritize evidence-producing steps, distinguish infrastructure health from scientific acceptance, and use event-bound waits instead of unchanged prerequisite rechecks. The prospective trial still requires an independent task corpus, isolated runner, reviewed protocol/budget, and independent ratings where applicable. No external Mishe kernel or production dispatch policy is changed by this document.

See [study gap analysis](study-gap-analysis.md) for the measured repository limitations and comparative research. Public promotion of new results requires independent review; a reproducible audit is not a registered specialization win.
